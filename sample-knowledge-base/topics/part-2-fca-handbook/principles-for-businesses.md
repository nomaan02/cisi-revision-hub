## Tier 1 · Must know

There are **12 Principles**. A firm must:

1. **Integrity:** conduct its business with integrity.
2. **Skill, care and diligence**
3. **Management and control:** organise and control its affairs responsibly, with adequate risk management.
4. **Financial prudence:** keep adequate financial resources.
5. **Market conduct:** observe proper standards of market conduct.
6. **Customers' interests:** pay due regard to them and treat them fairly.
7. **Communications with clients:** clear, fair and not misleading.
8. **Conflicts of interest:** manage them fairly.
9. **Customers: relationships of trust:** take reasonable care that advice and discretionary decisions are suitable.
10. **Clients' assets:** protect them adequately.
11. **Relations with regulators:** be open and co-operative.
12. **Consumer Duty:** act to deliver good outcomes for retail customers (in force from 31 July 2023).

## Tier 2 · Should know

- Where the Consumer Duty applies, Principles 6 and 7 do not, because the Duty sets a higher standard.

## Tier 3 · Awareness only

- The Principles are high-level. The detailed rules sit in other Handbook sourcebooks (COBS, SYSC, CASS …).
