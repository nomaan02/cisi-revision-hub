# Who Regulates UK Financial Services

> **Sample topic.** Written for this public demo from publicly available information. It is not CISI material.

|:---|:---|
| **Part** | 1 · Regulatory Framework |

## Tier 1 · Must know

- The **Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (FSMA)** is the main framework law.
- The **Financial Services Act 2012** replaced the FSA with two regulators from **1 April 2013**:
  - **FCA**, the Financial Conduct Authority: conduct regulation for all firms, and prudential regulation for firms the PRA doesn't cover.
  - **PRA**, the Prudential Regulation Authority, part of the **Bank of England**: prudential regulation of banks, building societies, credit unions, insurers and major investment firms.
- Firms supervised by both are **dual-regulated**.

## Tier 2 · Should know

- The **Financial Policy Committee** (FPC) at the Bank of England looks at risks to the system as a whole (macroprudential), not individual firms.
- **FSMA 2023** gives the regulators the job of replacing retained EU law with UK rules.

## Tier 3 · Awareness only

- The history of the FSA (1997–2013): know it existed, don't learn the detail.
