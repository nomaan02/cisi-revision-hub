# Practice: UK Regulators

> **Sample questions** written for this demo, in the style of short-answer questions. Not past-paper material.

**Q1.** Which body is responsible for the prudential regulation of UK banks?

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

The **Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA)**, part of the Bank of England.
</details>

**Q2.** Name the Act that created the FCA and PRA, and the year they took over.

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

The **Financial Services Act 2012**. They took over from the FSA on **1 April 2013**.
</details>

**Q3.** What does "dual-regulated" mean?

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

A firm supervised by the **PRA for prudential** matters and by the **FCA for conduct**.
</details>
