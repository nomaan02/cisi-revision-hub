# Practice Scenario: Consumer Duty

> **Sample scenario** written for this demo. Not past-paper material.

A small investment platform charges an exit fee that is only shown on page 14 of its terms. Complaints are rising from older customers who say they didn't know about it.

**Using a structured answer (Issue → Rule → Application → Conclusion), explain the regulatory concern and what the firm should do.**

<details><summary>Answer outline</summary>

- **Issue:** customers may not understand a material charge, and they're being harmed when they leave.
- **Rule:** Principle 12 / the Consumer Duty, especially the *consumer understanding* and *price and value* outcomes. Principle 7 is replaced here by the Duty.
- **Application:** burying the fee doesn't support good customer understanding, and rising complaints from older customers suggest possible harm to vulnerable customers.
- **Conclusion:** make the fee prominent before customers commit, review whether it's fair value, consider redress, and record the action for the board's Consumer Duty report.
</details>
